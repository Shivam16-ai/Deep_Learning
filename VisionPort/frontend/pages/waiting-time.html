Task 15 Queue Estimation Methodology

Waiting time estimation computes the continuous dwell time of vehicles whose tracked centroids remain inside the configured intersection waiting zone while their instantaneous speed remains below stationary threshold (< 2.0 km/h). When a vehicle moves past the stopline, its accumulated waiting time is finalized and incorporated into overall traffic signal efficiency metrics.
